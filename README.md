# Recruiter-Report

### Power BI Project
This project analyzes recruitment data to track candidate submissions, hiring performance, recruiter productivity, and client engagement. A Power BI report was developed to visualize recruitment trends, candidate pipeline status, and hiring performance across clients and job divisions. The report helps stakeholders evaluate recruiter performance, monitor hiring progress, and make data-driven recruitment decisions.

### Tools Covered : Advanced Excel, Power BI, Power Query, DAX

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

• Total Submissions

• Total Hires

• Year-to-Date (YTD)

• Month-to-Date (MTD)

• Total Submissions vs. Hires by Recruiter

• Total Submissions vs. Hires by Client

• Candidate Pipeline

• Job Status Distribution

• Monthly Submissions and Hires

• Total Hires by Job Division

• External Submissions by Job Division

• Total Submissions by Job Division

• Top 10 Recruiters by Submissions

• Submissions by Job Company

• Submissions by Recruiter

• Client-wise Submissions

• Month-wise Total Submissions

• Total Interviews by Organization

• Top 10 Clients by Total Submissions
